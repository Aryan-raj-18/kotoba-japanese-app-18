About Us

Kotoba (ことば — "language", "words") is a Japanese study app covering hiragana, katakana, kanji (including a full N5–N1 JLPT breakdown), vocabulary, verbs, stories, and daily practice with streaks. An local profile is optional — creating one separates streaks, review history, favorites and progress by username on this device. Everything still works without signing in, and Backup & Restore can move progress between installs.

What's inside

Three scripts

Full hiragana and katakana charts, and kanji lessons with on-yomi, kun-yomi, stroke counts, and real vocabulary.

JLPT N5–N1

{{ jlpt_total }} kanji organized by JLPT level, each with its own meaning/reading list and a 4-option practice quiz with easy/medium/hard tiers.

Kanji & JLPT practice quizzes

4-option multiple choice, no feedback until the round ends — then a full breakdown of every question and answer.

Writing Practice

Trace any kana or kanji inside its practice square and get a read on how closely your strokes matched.

Everyday language

Hundreds of daily-life words and phrases, {{ verb_total }} verbs with every conjugated form, and full dialogues.

Graded stories

Short stories with hiragana, romaji and English on tap, comprehension questions, and a grammar breakdown.

Practice & flashcards

Easy, medium, hard or mixed rounds. Learn a set of words, then complete real sentences with them.

Adaptive review & mistake notebook

Daily Review schedules weak items sooner, tracks mastery from New to Mastered, and keeps a notebook of mistakes until you recover them.

Listening & speaking

Japanese playback, slow shadowing, dialogue role practice, and microphone-based speaking practice with recognition when the device supports it.

Grammar, placement & JLPT mocks

A searchable N5–N1 study grammar dictionary, a diagnostic placement test, advanced roadmap chapters, and source-backed timed JLPT mock sessions.

Japan Explorer

An animated 47-prefecture map with prefectural-capital stops, offline illustrated postcards, local culture and food notes, level-aware Japanese words, audio, passport stamps, and mini quizzes that feed Smart Review.

Local profiles & Dashboard

Optionally create a local profile to separate streaks, XP, review history and progress by username. Export a portable backup when you want to move that state to another installation.

A note on the material

The vocabulary and kanji lessons are adapted from a beginner Japanese textbook and hand-curated everyday phrases. They're here for learning, not for redistribution.
