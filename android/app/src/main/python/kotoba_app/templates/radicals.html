How radicals work

1 · Meaning hint

氵 (water) in 海 sea, 洗 wash, 泳 swim. Same radical, all about water.

2 · Squashed forms

Some radicals change shape on the side of a kanji: 人→亻, 水→氵, 心→忄, 手→扌, 犬→犭. Same meaning, thinner body.

3 · Position

Radicals sit in set places: hen left, tsukuri right, kanmuri top, ashi bottom, tare top-left cover, nyō wraps left-bottom, kamae enclosure.

4 · Sound hint

The other half of a kanji often hints at the sound. 青 is sei, so 静 晴 清 精 are all read sei.
